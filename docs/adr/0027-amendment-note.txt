> Amended by [ADR-0029](0029-browser-upload-host-side-confined-conversion.md) (accepted by the owner, <date>): browser upload with host-side confined conversion.
> - **Profile.** The converter's profile no longer allows unrestricted `sysctl-read`: it allows an allow-list of hardware, VM and OS-identity names, with nothing under `kern.proc` or `kern.procargs`, and it refuses process-info about any process but itself (`(deny process-info*) (allow process-info* (target self))`). Measured in spikes/ingest-sysctl: under the earlier profile a confined child read another same-uid process's arguments, environment and cwd; under the amended one every channel tried is refused, and the converter's tests pass.
> - **Receive.** `reader.ingest.receive` now also accepts an uploaded material, not only a material declared in reader.yaml: only under a live claim of that upload (`--upload-id`, `--claim`) whose sha256 equals the artifact's `material_sha256` (SRV-INGEST FR-INGEST-23).
> - **"No new capability" restated.** The confined child gains nothing; the profile edit removes a capability it had (reading other same-uid processes' arguments, environment and cwd). The new host watcher (`reader-ingest --watch`), not the child, gains a long poll and byte fetch over `docker exec` into the worker; it holds no secret and opens no path the stack names.
> - **Start.** Ingestion of an uploaded source is started from the browser; `reader-ingest <material id>` for reader.yaml materials stays as it is.
